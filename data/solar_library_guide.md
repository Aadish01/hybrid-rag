# Solar-Powered Community Library

## Overview
Cedar Falls opened a solar-powered community library in April 2025. It uses rooftop solar panels and battery storage for lighting, public computers, Wi-Fi, and an air-quality sensor.

## Energy System
The library has 24 solar panels rated at 400 watts each. The total installed capacity is 9.6 kW. On a clear day, the system typically generates 38 kWh.

The battery has a usable capacity of 20 kWh. The library reserves 5 kWh for emergency lighting and networking, leaving 15 kWh for routine evening use.

## Operating Hours
The library is open Tuesday through Sunday, from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. It is closed on Mondays. Public computers shut down at 6:45 PM.

## Internet and Privacy
Guest Wi-Fi is free, and its access token rotates every 24 hours. Staff devices use a separate network isolated by a firewall. Guests do not need to provide their names to use Wi-Fi. Public computers clear browser history and downloaded files after each session.

## Maintenance
Staff inspect the solar inverter on the first Tuesday of each month. The battery management system reports a warning when battery temperature exceeds 42°C. This warning does not automatically mean the battery shuts down; staff must check the system and follow the manufacturer's safety procedure.
